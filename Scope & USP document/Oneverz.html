Release 1 – End-to-End Business Journey

Platform Admin creates tenant → assigns Trial / Demo subscription → activates tenant → Tenant Admin configures Outlet, Till, Roles, Users, Products and Opening Stock → customer purchases through Physical POS or Online Store → Click & Collect fulfilment → Inventory updated → Reports & Business Insights.

Exclusions from Release 1

Online Subscription Payment, Automatic Subscription Renewal, Card Payment, LankaQR, Split Payment, Full Delivery / Shipping E-Commerce, Full 14/14 Inventory Suite, Stock Transfer, Stock Count, Advanced Purchase / Supplier Management, Advanced Billing and Advanced BI / Forecasting.
