OneXso Differentiators (USP)
01

ZERO-TOUCH INTELLIGENT WORKDAY (USP)

Automatic capture: the workday is captured with less manual logging.

Complete timeline: attendance, apps, meetings and documents are connected.

02

FLEXIBLE WORK & TASK MANAGEMENT (USP)

Any work structure: unlimited hierarchy and dynamic inheritance / roll-up.

Outcome focus: estimates, progress and tasks can sit anywhere in the tree.

03

ACTUAL VS. REPORTED WORK INTELLIGENCE (USP)

Verified activity: system-captured work is compared with employee updates and time logs.

Fairer accountability: gaps and bottlenecks become visible instead of assumed.

04

EXCEPTION-BASED LEADERSHIP (USP)

Attention only where needed: overdue tasks, attendance issues, unusual inactivity, policy violations and leave conflicts.

Faster decisions: leaders focus on exceptions instead of reviewing everything.

05

360° WORKFORCE COMMAND CENTRE (USP)

One platform: role-based dashboards and organization-wide visibility.

Governance: consistent control, security and scalability across the organization.
