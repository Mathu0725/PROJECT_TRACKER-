EXECUTIVE THESIS

Greyhound turns the race day into a pocket pass.

Discover → book → unlock → enter. Tickets, entitlements and alerts live in one mobile experience — even when the network drops at the gate.

Greyhound Differentiators (USP)
01

OFFLINE-FIRST RACE-DAY PASS (USP)

Works at the gate: ticket UI and entitlements stay usable when connectivity is weak.

Confidence on entry: fans and staff are not blocked by a dead signal at the venue.

02

QR SCAN → INSTANT WALLET (USP)

Import in seconds: scan a QR token and land a usable ticket in My Tickets.

Entitlement clarity: usage and booking meta stay attached to the pass.

03

SECURE APP LOCK LAYER (USP)

Fan-owned device security: PIN, pattern, password or biometrics protect the wallet.

Trust for high-value passes: tickets stay locked until the owner unlocks.

04

INVITE & LOOKUP FLOW (USP)

Share the race: invitations move guests from message to landing to attendance.

Booking recovery: lookup finds entitlements without forcing a full repurchase path.

05

ONE SHELL — FULL RACE JOURNEY (USP)

Single app surface: Home, Events, Tickets, Alerts and Settings in one navigation.

Race-day readiness: checklist, notifications and support sit beside the pass.
