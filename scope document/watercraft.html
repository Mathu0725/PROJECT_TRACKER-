Watercraft — Core Scope Areas
01

Signup, Login & Password Reset

Customers access the portal using existing OPTIMO-linked accounts and can reset passwords when required.

02

Watercraft Storage Booking

Select Dinghy, Kayak or Wharf Tie-Up storage, choose venue and berth/rack, review and submit a booking request.

03

Real-Time Availability

Shows available spaces, capacity, berth/rack details, dimensions, filters and relevant pricing from backend data.

04

Online Waitlist

Customers can join the waitlist when no suitable space is available, with council review and availability notifications.

05

Permit Renewal

View active permits, select eligible permits, review renewal summary, pay online and receive confirmation.

06

Permit Release

Customers can release selected permits or all permits online when they do not wish to renew.

07

BPOINT Integration

Secure online payment gateway integration for permit renewal payments and related confirmation processing.

08

Email & Renewal Reminders

Booking confirmations, waitlist updates, renewal reminders, payment confirmations and customer communications.

09

Booking Summary & Uploads

Review selected storage details and upload required supporting documents such as proof of address where applicable.

10

NBC Theme

Northern Beaches Council themed customer portal experience.

CORE VALUE PROPOSITION

One customer self-service journey for watercraft storage.

Real-time availability, booking, waitlist, permit renewal, permit release, payment and customer communication are brought together in one portal.

Watercraft Differentiators (USP)
01

REAL-TIME STORAGE AVAILABILITY (USP)

Immediate visibility: customers can see available watercraft storage locations and suitable berth/rack options before submitting a request.

Better decisions: backend-driven capacity and storage details reduce unnecessary enquiries.

02

END-TO-END ONLINE BOOKING EXPERIENCE (USP)

One journey: storage selection, venue choice, berth/rack selection, booking review, document upload and request submission are handled online.

Less manual handling: customers complete more of the process without staff intervention.

03

INTEGRATED ONLINE WAITLIST (USP)

Digital waitlist: when no suitable space is available, customers can join the waitlist directly online.

Improved service: reduces reliance on manual email-based requests and supports availability notifications.

04

SELF-SERVICE PERMIT RENEWAL & RELEASE (USP)

Customer control: permit holders can renew eligible permits or release storage spaces online.

Operational efficiency: reduces repetitive staff processing for common permit actions.

05

SECURE PAYMENT & MAPPING REMINDERS (USP)

BPOINT payment: supports secure online renewal payments and confirmation processing.

Timely communication: automated renewal reminders help customers act before renewal deadlines.
